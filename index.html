Sawtooth setup guide

Sawtooth is a Shopify theme for hardware, tools, and industrial-supply stores. This guide covers the setup steps that the theme editor cannot do for you. Everything else is a setting or a section you can add in Online Store > Themes > Customize.

1. Pick a style

Sawtooth ships with four styles. Choose one when you add the theme, or switch later in Theme settings. Each style changes fonts, colors, corner radius, and text casing.

StyleLook
SawtoothLight, with a burnt-orange accent
FoundryDark, with a safety-yellow accent
RivetLight and rounded, with a blue accent
HazardBlack and yellow, high contrast

All color schemes in every style meet a 4.5:1 text contrast ratio. If you change a color, check contrast with any WCAG contrast checker.

2. Turn on filters

Collection filters, the product finder, and complementary products come from the free Shopify Search & Discovery app.

  1. Install Search & Discovery from the Shopify App Store.
  2. Go to Filters and add the filters your customers shop by. For hardware stores, start with Availability, Price, Vendor (brand), Product type, and any spec metafields such as size or voltage.
  3. In Product recommendations, set complementary products. They appear in the product page Complementary products block and in the cart upsell.

The Product finder section shows one dropdown per filter of the collection you pick. Without filters it shows a note in the editor only.

3. Add product specifications

The spec table, product tabs, and product comparison read product metafields in one namespace. The default namespace is specs. To use another one, change Theme settings > Product cards > Specification metafield namespace (tabs and comparison) and the Namespace setting of the Specifications block.

  1. Go to Settings > Custom data > Products and add a definition for each spec, for example specs.voltage (Single line text) or specs.weight_capacity (Weight).
  2. Fill the values on each product.

Every specs.* metafield then shows in the Specifications block, the Tabs block, and the comparison table, labeled with the definition's name. To add rows by hand instead, write them in the Specifications block settings.

Optional metafields:

  • Spec sheet: add a File or URL metafield and connect it to the Spec sheet block with the dynamic source button.
  • Ratings: a reviews app that writes reviews.rating and reviews.rating_count shows stars on cards and the product page.

4. Set up the special pages

Create a page in Online Store > Pages, then pick its template:

TemplateWhat it shows
page.tradeTrade account sign-up with a bulk-order form
page.quoteRequest-a-quote form that includes the customer's cart
page.brandsA to Z list of every vendor, linked to their products
page.locationsStore locator with a map that loads only on request
page.compareFull product comparison table
page.showcaseAbout page with banner, collage, delivery and returns tabs, and team
page.contactContact form with custom fields

Then link the quote page in Theme settings > Cart > Quote request page to show a Request a quote button in the cart.

5. B2B and bulk ordering

  • Volume pricing: the Volume pricing block shows quantity price breaks for B2B customers whose catalog has them. It needs Shopify B2B.
  • Quick order list: the Quick order block lists every variant with a quantity field. It shows on products with more than one variant.
  • Bulk order: the Bulk order section lets customers enter or paste SKUs with quantities and add them all to the cart. Give every variant a SKU.
  • Quantity rules: minimums, maximums, and increments from B2B catalogs show next to the quantity selector.

6. Shipping and delivery messages

  • Delivery estimate block: set the daily cut-off time, the days you ship, and the minimum and maximum delivery days. The countdown uses your store's time zone from Settings > General.
  • Free shipping bar: set Free shipping goal in Theme settings > Cart. Use the same amount as your free shipping rate.
  • Shipping estimator: turn it on in Theme settings > Cart. It uses your real shipping rates.

7. Navigation

  • Mega menu: add a second and third level of links under a main menu item.
  • Sub-category chips: on collection pages, the chips come from the links nested under that collection in the menu you choose in the collection section settings.

8. Popups

The newsletter popup and the age check are sections. Add them to any template and set the delay and pages in their settings. A dismissed popup stays closed for the number of days you choose.

9. Design options

  • Card hover effect: in Theme settings > Product cards, choose zoom image, lift card, or none. Visitors who prefer reduced motion see no animation.
  • Collection banner: in the collection page section, set Image style to Banner behind the title and pick a color scheme that reads well on your images.
  • Promo tiles in the product grid: add a Promo tile block to the collection page section and choose its position. Tiles show on the first page only and hide while filters are applied.
  • Heading tags: most sections have a Heading tag setting. Use H1 once per page for the main heading, for example on the first slideshow slide.
  • Footer: add a Brand block for your logo, text, and social icons. Turn the safety stripe on or off in the footer settings.
  • Cart message: set Checkout message in Theme settings > Cart to show your returns or warranty promise below the checkout button.

10. Customer accounts

Sawtooth uses Shopify's new customer accounts. The account icon in the header and the mobile menu appears once you turn on Settings > Customer accounts > Show sign-in links. If your store still uses legacy customer accounts, upgrade on the same page first: Sawtooth has no legacy account page templates.

11. Languages

Storefront text is translated into English, French, German, Spanish, and Italian. Edit any text in Online Store > Themes > ... > Edit default theme content.

Frequently asked questions

Why don't filters show on my collection pages?

Filters come from the free Shopify Search & Discovery app. Install it, then add filters under Filters in the app. They appear on collection and search pages without any theme changes. See section 2.

Why is the specifications table empty?

The table reads product metafields in the specs namespace. Add definitions in Settings > Custom data > Products and fill the values on each product, or type rows by hand in the Specifications block. See section 3.

My product photos look cropped on cards. How do I show the whole photo?

Go to Theme settings > Product cards and set Image fit to Fit. Use Fill when your photos have busy backgrounds and you want cards to fill edge to edge.

Why don't I see the account icon in the header?

Turn on Settings > Customer accounts > Show sign-in links. Sawtooth works with Shopify's new customer accounts. See section 10.

Why doesn't the delivery estimate show on some products?

It shows only for variants that are in stock and can ship. Sold-out and backordered variants hide it. Also check that the block has a cut-off time and at least one shipping day set.

Can I add apps to Sawtooth?

Yes. Add app blocks to the product page and the featured product section, or add the Apps section to any page to place app content anywhere.

Does Sawtooth work with Shopify B2B?

Yes. Volume pricing, quantity rules, and B2B catalogs show automatically for B2B customers. See section 5.

How do I update Sawtooth?

When a new version is released, Shopify shows an update notice on the theme in Online Store > Themes. The update keeps your settings and content.

Support policy

We help with:

  • Setting up and using Sawtooth's features and settings
  • Bugs in the theme's own code
  • Questions about this documentation

We can't help with:

  • Custom code changes or new features built for one store
  • Third-party apps and their settings
  • Problems caused by edits to the theme's code

We reply within two business days, Monday to Friday. Send your store URL, the page where you see the problem, and a screenshot if you can. Fixes for confirmed bugs ship in the next theme update.

Support

Use the support form. Your Sawtooth version is in Theme settings > Theme info.
