Get help with Anvil

Check the setup guide first; most questions are answered there. If you're still stuck, send us a message. We reply within two business days.

Open the support form

The form opens in a new tab. You can attach a screenshot, and you get a copy of your message by email. Attaching a file needs a Google sign-in.

Support policy

We help with setting up Anvil's features and settings, bugs in the theme's own code, and questions about the documentation. We can't help with custom code changes, third-party apps, or problems caused by edits to the theme's code.

We reply within two business days, Monday to Friday. Include your store URL, the page where you see the problem, and a screenshot if you can.
